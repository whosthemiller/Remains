Remains is an archival website exploring abandoned digital photo albums and slow wandering through lost digital memories

Scroll to explore

Remains explores abandoned digital photo albums as quiet traces of memory. It focuses on images that were once carefully collected and publicly shared, then left inactive — not deleted, but still remaining online.

I began this project from a place of information overload. Rather than reducing or resolving the excess, I chose to stay with it — to look for what might still be good inside.

By allowing images to gather and persist within the same space, Remains invites slow wandering rather than search or resolution, emphasizing time, accumulation, and the weight of what has been left behind.

Thank you to everyone who shared their images and allowed them to remain.

All images are sourced from public albums on Flickr.
